Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Saver Market collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our website, create an account, sign in with Google, place an order, or contact us.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and account identifiers.
  • Order and delivery information: delivery address, products ordered, quantities, order status, and order history.
  • Google Sign-In information: if you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier as provided through Google and our authentication provider.
  • Website information: browser/device information, login session data, cart, wishlist, language preference, and similar information required for the website to function.
  • Communications: information you send through forms, email, or WhatsApp.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your Saver Market account.
  • To authenticate you and protect account security.
  • To process, manage, and display orders and order history.
  • To send account verification, password-reset, order, and customer-support communications.
  • To remember your cart, wishlist, language, and website preferences.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve Saver Market.

3. Google user data

When you use “Continue with Google,” Saver Market uses Google OAuth only for account sign-in and identification. We request only the basic information needed to create or access your Saver Market account, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier.

We do not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or your Google password.

4. Service providers

We use trusted service providers to operate Saver Market, including Supabase for authentication and database services, Google for optional Google Sign-In, Resend for authentication email delivery, Cloudflare for hosting/security/performance, and WhatsApp when you choose to communicate with us there. These providers process information according to their own terms and policies.

5. Payments

Saver Market currently uses cash on delivery where ordering is available. We do not collect or store payment-card details through the website unless a different payment method is clearly introduced in the future.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We may use cookies and browser storage such as local storage to keep you signed in, maintain your cart and wishlist, remember language preferences, support installed web-app features, and protect account security.

7. Data retention

We keep account, order, and related information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security and business records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable requirements. Information may be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so customers should also protect their passwords and devices.

9. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about your personal information, request corrections, or request deletion of your Saver Market account and associated personal data where applicable. Some order or transaction records may need to be retained for legitimate business, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons.

10. Children

Saver Market is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to create accounts or make purchases under applicable law without appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy or account questions, contact Saver Market through the Contact section of our website or at saver.marketing9@gmail.com.
